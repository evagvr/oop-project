# Behind the Fame
## Description:
MusicLife is a game where you take control over the choices and activities a music band makes, while your mission is to make the band known.
## Gameplay:
The game starts with the player building the band: choosing strategically the members and its personnel(the manager and the producer). The player has to develop members' skills with certain activities such as reherarsals, album recordings, modifying the structure of the band(adding new members or removing existing members), concerts and band tours.
## The end of the game
**Win** by getting the popularity score to 100

**OR**

**Lose** if the band runs out if money, the members leave the band or the time runs out (you have 20 years to get your band to popularity)
